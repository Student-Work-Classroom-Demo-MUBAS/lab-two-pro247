Martin "Martse" Nkhata

Doreen paying tribute to Martse

Martin Nkhata, Known acros Malawi as Martse was one of the country's most influnetial young voices in hip-hop, using his music to speak for a generation of Malawian youth.

Martse's mixtapeRaps and Dances, released in 2018, cemented his place as one of Malawi's leading lap artists.

He began his music journey around 2004, drawing early inspiration from Dzimbiri (Mphatso Katopola) of the group Khamradzi.

Beyond music, Martse ran a "bring a desk to school" project, supplying desks to primary school learners who needed them.
